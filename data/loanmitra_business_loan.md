# HDFC Bank Business Loan - Public Eligibility Criteria

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL for the complete document and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## Publicly listed criteria

HDFC Bank's Business Loan eligibility page states the following for eligible self-employed applicants and entities operating in manufacturing, trading or services:

- Nationality: Indian.
- Applicant age: at least 21 at application; not older than 65 at loan maturity.
- Minimum annual business turnover: ₹40 lakh.
- Minimum annual income shown in ITR: ₹3 lakh.
- At least three years in the current business and five years of total business experience.
- Business profitable for the previous two years.
- Eligible forms listed include self-employed individuals, proprietors, private limited companies and partnership firms; the main product page also lists LLPs.

## Important limits

HDFC says approvals are at its sole discretion and subject to documentation and verification. These are published criteria for the described Business Loan product, not necessarily requirements for every HDFC MSME product, secured facility, professional loan, or government-backed scheme. The page does not supply a universal minimum CIBIL score.

# HDFC Bank Business Loan - Product Features, Collateral and Tenure

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## Collateral and guarantor

The HDFC Bank Business Loan is an unsecured, collateral-free loan. The page states loan amounts of up to ₹75 lakh, and up to ₹1 crore in select locations, without any collateral or guarantor required. Because the loan is unsecured, HDFC states its interest rate is higher than for secured products.

## Loan amount and tenure

- Loan amount: up to ₹75 lakh, and up to ₹1 crore in select locations.
- Repayment tenure: 12 to 48 months.
- Eligibility check and loan approval: the page advertises a decision within 60 seconds for eligibility checks.

## Balance transfer

An existing business loan from another lender can be transferred to HDFC Bank, with a tenure of up to 48 months for the transferred balance.

## Dropline Overdraft facility

HDFC offers a Dropline Overdraft (DOD) facility without security: a limit of ₹1 lakh to ₹25 lakh is set in a separate current account, and the limit reduces monthly until the end of the tenure (12 to 48 months). Interest is paid only on the amount used. No guarantor or security is required.

## Credit Protect

The loan package can include Credit Protect, a life-cover plan.

## How to apply

Applications can be made through HDFC's digital application, MobileBanking, NetBanking or a branch.

## Interest rate display on this page

The product page shows several different rate figures: a banner of "12.75% p.a. onwards", a section titled "Starting from 16.85%", and an EMI calculator slider from 9.99% to 24%. The official rack range on the bank's interest-rates-and-charges page is 10.75% to 22.50%; use that page (see hdfc_business_loan_rates_and_charges) for rate questions.

# HDFC Bank Business Loan - Interest Rates, Fees and Charges

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## Interest rate

The HDFC Bank Business Loan rack interest rate range is a minimum of 10.75% and a maximum of 22.50% per annum. The rate an individual borrower receives is set in their own sanction letter and Key Facts Statement.

For loans disbursed between April 2026 and June 2026 (Q1 2026-27), the page reports these actual rates offered, with interest type Fixed:

- Internal rate of return (IRR): minimum 9.50%, maximum 21.03%, average 16.73%. (The page labels the 21.03% row "Min IRR"; from context it is the maximum.)
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR), which includes fees: minimum 9.53%, maximum 26.32%, average 17.30%.

## Processing fee and stamp duty

- Loan processing charges: up to 2.00% of the loan amount, plus applicable government taxes.
- Processing fee is NIL for loan facilities up to ₹5 lakh availed by micro and small enterprises, subject to submission of the Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) before disbursal.
- Stamp duty and other statutory charges: as per the applicable laws of the state.
- Senior citizen customers receive a 10% discount on all service charges.

## Foreclosure and prepayment (premature closure) charges

Premature closure charges for a term loan, on part or full repayment, are calculated on principal outstanding:

- Up to 24 EMIs paid: 4% of principal outstanding.
- After 24 and up to 36 EMIs paid: 3% of principal outstanding.
- More than 36 EMIs paid: 2% of principal outstanding.

Part prepayment is allowed after the first EMI has been paid, up to 25% of principal outstanding, only once in a financial year and at most twice during the loan tenure. The same percentage charges apply to the part-prepayment amount.

There are NIL premature closure charges for fixed-rate loan facilities up to ₹50 lakh availed by micro and small enterprises. Government taxes apply in addition to these charges.

To foreclose, the borrower registers a service request for Business Loan foreclosure, or visits the nearest Retail Loan Service Centre.

## Late or missed EMI

If an EMI is paid after its due date, interest is charged on the unpaid EMI for the number of days it is late. This interest is calculated at the loan's contracted rate and added to the next EMI. A payment return (bounced payment) is charged ₹450 per instance.

## Other charges

- Loan closure letter and duplicate loan closure letter: NIL.
- Repayment mode change: ₹500.
- Physical copy of repayment / amortisation schedule: ₹50 per schedule (free to download from the website).
- Legal, repossession and incidental charges: at actuals.
- Loan cancellation is allowed within the cooling-off / look-up period from disbursal. The borrower pays interest from disbursal to cancellation; processing fee, stamp duty, statutory charges and GST are not refunded. Rebooking charge: ₹1,000 plus government taxes.

## Fixed and floating interest rates

The page defines a floating rate as an interest rate that is allowed to move up and down with the rest of the market or along with an index, and a fixed rate as an interest rate that remains at a predetermined rate for the entire term of the loan. The Q1 2026-27 Business Loan rates on this page are listed as interest type Fixed. Charges for switching between fixed and floating rates are listed as "Not applicable".

## Dropline Overdraft (DOD) facility charges

- Processing fee: up to 2% of the loan amount (NIL up to ₹5 lakh for micro and small enterprises with URC).
- Interest on amounts used above the available limit: 24% per annum.
- Premature closure after the three-day cooling-off period: 4% of the existing operating limit in months 0-24, 3% in months 25-36, and 2% after 36 months. NIL for fixed-rate facilities up to ₹50 lakh for micro and small enterprises.
- Non-utilisation charge: minimum expected utilisation is 30% of the limit. 0.10% is charged quarterly on the shortfall, up to a maximum of ₹5,000.
- Overdraft statement / repayment schedule: ₹500 per statement.

# HDFC Bank Business Loan - Required Documentation

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. ## Documents listed by HDFC Bank

- PAN card for the company, firm or individual applicant.
- Identity proof: any one of Aadhaar card, passport, Voter ID, PAN card or driving licence.
- Address proof: any one of Aadhaar card, passport, Voter ID or driving licence.
- Bank statements for the previous six months.
- Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) with computation of income.
- Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the previous two years, CA-certified/audited.
- Proof of business continuation: ITR, trade licence, or establishment/sales-tax certificate.
- Business-constitution documents as applicable: sole-proprietorship declaration; certified partnership deed; or company Memorandum & Articles of Association certified by a director, or original board resolution.

The broader HDFC Business Loan page also lists the last six months of GST returns as income proof in its overview. Product and legal-constitution requirements may vary, so the current application checklist is authoritative for an individual application.

HDFC notes that approval is subject to documentation and verification as per the bank's requirements.
