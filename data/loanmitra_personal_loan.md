# HDFC Bank Personal Loan - Public Eligibility and Product Facts

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL for the complete document and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## Publicly listed eligibility information

The HDFC Bank Personal Loan page states the following eligibility criteria:

- Age: 21 to 60 years.
- Employment: employees of private, limited and LLP companies, and employees of public-sector undertakings (central, state and local bodies).
- Work experience: at least two years of total work experience, including at least one year with the current organisation.
- Income: minimum monthly net income of ₹25,000.

## Product details displayed on HDFC's main personal-loan page

The main product page displayed a loan range of ₹25,000 to ₹50 lakh and an advertised fixed interest rate range of 9.99% to 24.00% per annum. It also displayed processing charges up to ₹6,500 plus GST and said stamp duty is charged at actuals, subject to applicable state law. These are page-displayed commercial terms and should be rechecked live before being used as current quotes.

The page says pre-approved HDFC customers may receive funds in about 10 seconds, while other customers may take up to four working days subject to required documentation and verification. Final disbursal remains at the bank's discretion and terms apply.

## What this source does not establish

- It does not establish a universal minimum CIBIL score of 700.
- It does not guarantee approval when all listed criteria are met.
- It does not replace the individual offer, sanction letter, KFS, MITC, or agreement for a borrower.

# HDFC Bank Personal Loan - Interest Rates, Fees and Charges

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## Interest rate

The HDFC Bank Personal Loan rack interest rate for salaried customers ranges from 9.99% to 24.00% per annum. The rate an individual borrower receives is set in their own sanction letter and Key Facts Statement.

For loans disbursed between April 2026 and June 2026 (Q1 2026-27), the page reports these actual rates offered:

- Internal rate of return (IRR): minimum 9.97%, maximum 24.06%, average 10.89%.
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR), which includes fees: minimum 9.99%, maximum 33.94%, average 11.18%.

## Processing fee and stamp duty

- Processing fee: up to ₹6,500 plus GST.
- Stamp duty and other statutory charges: as per the applicable laws of the state.
- Senior citizen customers receive a 10% discount on all service charges.
- Government taxes and levies are added to all fees and charges.

## Foreclosure (full prepayment) charges

Premature closure charges for full repayment apply on principal outstanding after the cooling-off / look-up period:

- Up to 24 EMIs paid: 4% of principal outstanding.
- After 24 and up to 36 EMIs paid: 3% of principal outstanding.
- After 36 EMIs paid: 2% of principal outstanding.

To foreclose, the borrower registers a service request for Personal Loan foreclosure (an online token can be raised).

## Part prepayment charges

Part prepayment is allowed after the first EMI has been paid, up to 25% of principal outstanding, only once in a financial year and at most twice during the loan tenure. Charges apply on the part-payment amount:

- After 1 EMI and up to 24 EMIs paid: 4% of the part-payment amount.
- After 24 and up to 36 EMIs paid: 3% of the part-payment amount.
- After 36 EMIs paid: 2% of the part-payment amount.

## Late or missed EMI

If an EMI is paid after its due date, interest is charged on the unpaid EMI for the number of days it is late, calculated at the loan's contracted rate and added to the next EMI. Example from the page: EMI due on the 10th and paid on the 25th means 15 days of interest is added to the next EMI. A payment return (bounced payment) is charged ₹450 per instance.

## Other charges

- Repayment mode change: ₹500.
- Physical copy of repayment / amortisation schedule: ₹50 per schedule (free to download from the website).
- Legal and incidental charges: at actuals.
- Loan cancellation is allowed within the cooling-off / look-up period from disbursal. The borrower pays interest from disbursal to cancellation; processing fee, stamp duty, statutory charges and GST are not refunded. Rebooking charge: ₹1,000 plus government taxes.

# HDFC Bank Personal Loan - Documentation Notes

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL for the complete document and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

HDFC Bank's general Personal Loan Documentation page says that KYC-compliant bank / pre-approved customers may avail the product with no documentation as part of a digital journey. It also notes that approvals remain subject to the bank's documentation and verification requirements.

A separate HDFC salaried-person product page has described a route-dependent checklist for non-pre-approved applicants: the last three months of bank statements, two latest salary slips, and KYC. This was shown on a page marked updated 23 October 2024. Because HDFC's product journeys differ by customer and offer, do not make one checklist universal.

For a chatbot, ask the user whether they are pre-approved and whether they are salaried or self-employed, then direct them to the current application-specific checklist. Avoid asking users to upload real identity or financial documents into a demo chatbot.
