# HDFC Bank Credit Card - General Eligibility and Card-Specific Criteria

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL for the complete document and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

## General criteria shown on HDFC's credit-card overview

HDFC Bank's general credit-card page says:

- Age: at least 18 years.
- Nationality: Indian national.
- Residential status: Resident / Non-Resident Indian.
- Employment status: salaried or self-employed.
- Annual income: HDFC Bank determines eligibility and the subsequent credit limit based on annual income.

## Card-specific criteria can differ

Individual card pages may use higher age and income thresholds. For example, the HDFC Regalia eligibility page lists salaried Indian nationals aged 21-60 with net monthly income above ₹1 lakh, and self-employed Indian nationals aged 21-65 with ITR above ₹12 lakh per year. Other cards have different requirements.

## What to do in the chatbot

Ask which card/product the applicant is interested in and apply only that card's official criteria. Do not combine the general minimum age from the overview with the income threshold of a different card. Do not present a credit-score threshold as HDFC policy unless the selected card's current official source explicitly states it.

The linked PDF is the HDFC Bank **Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC)** document. The indexed version states it is to be read with the Card Member Agreement and that MITC terms are subject to revision. It is 46 pages and covers, among other areas:

- Card-specific joining and annual fees.
- Finance charges and how the minimum amount due is calculated.
- Billing, payment, interest-free periods and payment allocation.
- Fees and charges (which can vary by card variant and offer).
- Surrender/closure, lost or stolen cards, and dispute / customer-service procedures.
- Additional terms for business credit cards.

The MITC is the preferred bank source for current fees and product terms; do not lift a charge from a historic cardmember agreement without checking whether the latest MITC revises it. Because the source PDF is not embedded here, this is an extract/summary and the official PDF remains the full source.

# HDFC Bank Credit Card Cardmember Agreement - Source and Summary

> **Provenance note:** This is a source-linked, retrieval-friendly text extract/summary prepared from the official page/document identified below. It is not a replacement for the complete official original. Use the official source URL for the complete document and check for amendments before relying on numerical terms.

The linked HDFC Bank Cardmember Agreement describes the contractual relationship for HDFC Bank international credit cards, including definitions, information disclosure, credit limits, transactions, balance transfers, instalment facilities, payments, default and termination.

The indexed agreement states that changes in schedules of fees and charges and terms and conditions will normally have prospective effect with 30 days' notice (except changes resulting from regulatory requirements). It also describes balance-transfer and over-limit terms, but the current MITC and individual card terms should be checked before using numerical amounts as current.

This document is useful for post-issuance card-use questions. It is not the credit-card application eligibility policy.
